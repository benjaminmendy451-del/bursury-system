Privacy

How BursaryNexa handles your information.

A demonstration system

BursaryNexa is a student project that shows how a bursary system can work. Please enter test information only while it is running as a demonstration.

What we collect

  • Your name, ID or birth certificate number, phone number and email address.
  • Your county, ward, school or college and admission number.
  • The facts used to score need: family income, number of dependants, parental status, disability and fees.
  • The documents you upload (ID, fee statement, admission letter, proof of income).

Why we collect it

Only to decide your bursary application, to tell you the result, and to give the committee totals for planning. Scores are a guide, and the committee makes the decision.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own application and documents.
  • Committee members can see applications and documents so they can review them. Every change they make is recorded in an audit log.
  • Uploaded documents are never public. They open only after login, for the owner or the committee.
  • Public pages show no personal details. The award check page shows only a first name and the first letter of the last name.

How it is protected

Pages are served over HTTPS, passwords are stored in a scrambled (hashed) form, repeated failed logins are slowed down, and secret settings are kept out of the code.

Your choices

You can change your details from your dashboard. To ask the committee to correct or remove your information, use the contact details on the home page. The design follows the ideas of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, such as collecting only what is needed and keeping it secure.
